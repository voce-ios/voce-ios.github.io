Voce Usage and Subscription Information

Last updated: 2026-11-08

1. Developer and license

Voce is developed by Ahmet Burak Yıldırım. For support and app-related questions, contact burak782@icloud.com.

For an app obtained through the App Store, Apple’s Standard End User License Agreement applies unless a separate custom license is provided. This page explains app usage and subscriptions; it is not a custom EULA replacing Apple’s license agreement. Apple Standard EULA.

2. Current version and your data

Voce offers a free plan and Voce Pro. On the free plan, the first 1 minute 30 seconds of each recording are transcribed, and the full audio is kept. Voce Pro transcribes every second of the recording. It also unlocks export as text, a PDF report, MP3 and M4A, and meeting summaries, study notes and action lists prepared on a supported device. The plan, period and price in your local currency are shown on the purchase screen before you confirm. This page is not a price quote.

Your recordings, transcripts and edits remain under your control. An expired subscription does not remove your ability to play or edit existing notes. All exports and sharing, including text, PDF reports and audio, require a valid Pro entitlement. Files already exported remain yours.

3. Purchases

  • Purchases are made through Apple’s App Store. The offered plan, period, local price and any trial conditions are shown in Apple’s purchase interface before confirmation.
  • Lifetime Pro is a one-time, non-consumable purchase with no subscription renewal or scheduled expiry. Apple refunds or revocations may remove that entitlement. It can be restored using the same Apple account. Buying lifetime access does not automatically cancel a separate monthly or yearly subscription; manage that subscription to stop future renewals.
  • An auto-renewable subscription renews for the applicable period and terms unless cancelled through your Apple account. Cancellation stops future renewal; access continues through the valid period. Apple refunds or revocations may affect access separately.
  • Manage subscriptions through iOS Settings → Apple Account → Subscriptions or the app’s Manage subscription button. Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription.
  • Restore purchases rechecks a valid entitlement associated with the same Apple account. Internet access may be required depending on the account, product and purchase status.
  • Payments and refund requests follow Apple’s applicable processes. The developer does not guarantee a refund outcome or a particular trial period. Apple refund information.

4. Offline scope and AI eligibility

Recording, transcription and local note operations run on your device. Purchases, restoration and renewals may require a network connection. Offline operation does not provide indefinite access after an entitlement expires.

Buying Pro does not add Apple Intelligence support to an incompatible device. System-model tools require eligible hardware, iOS version, language, Apple Intelligence settings and a ready system model. The bundled Whisper transcription model operates separately. Processing speed, memory, battery and storage use depend on your device and recording.

5. Review results and use responsibly

Transcription, speaker separation and AI drafts can contain errors. Check names, numbers, tasks and decisions against the source. These outputs are not provided as a substitute for professional advice or human judgment. Export important recordings to keep a separate copy.

Use only content you are authorized to record, import or share. Respect other people’s recording and privacy rights. Third-party services you choose for sharing have their own terms.

6. Privacy and contact

Data handling, backups, deletion and support correspondence are described in the separate Privacy Policy. You can read it from the app’s Offline use & privacy screen. Use the contact address above for support; sending sensitive recordings is not required for ordinary use.
