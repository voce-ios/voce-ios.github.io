Voce Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-11-08

1. Scope and contact

This policy describes data handling in the Voce iOS app and on the web page hosting this policy. The app developer and person/entity responsible for processing under their control is Ahmet Burak Yıldırım. For privacy questions, contact burak782@icloud.com.

Voce records audio, transcribes selected audio/video files, organizes notes, separates speakers and offers local AI tools on supported devices. You do not need an account for your recordings and notes.

2. Data processed on your device

Depending on the features you use, the app stores or processes the following on your device:

  • Audio you record; audio/video files you choose to import; and audio copies created for processing.
  • Original and edited transcripts, note titles, summaries, AI drafts, tasks, decisions, speaker labels, bookmarks and timestamps.
  • Folders, categories, colors, tags, favorites, vocabulary terms and editing history used to organize your notes.
  • Language, appearance and model preferences; note identifiers, creation dates, processing progress and local records needed for interruption recovery.

These are used to provide the recording, recognition, editing, search, report and recovery functions you choose. They are not automatically uploaded to a developer-operated account or content server.

3. Offline processing and AI

Transcription and automatic speaker separation run on your device using models bundled with the app. Voce does not send your audio or transcripts to a remote AI service for these functions. Your content is not collected to train the developer’s models.

Speaker separation attempts to group voices within a recording; it does not aim to discover a person’s real identity or name from their voice. You can assign names yourself. Results can be wrong.

Tools requiring Apple Intelligence run when Apple’s on-device Foundation Models model is ready on a supported device and in a supported language. iOS manages that system model. Voce does not switch these tools to a cloud AI fallback; a tool may be unavailable when the system model is not ready. Local AI output can also contain errors.

4. Permissions and local shared storage

Microphone permission is used for recording. You can revoke it in iOS Settings; revocation does not delete existing recordings. Import processes files you select or content you explicitly share from another app.

The main Voce app, its share extension and widget may exchange necessary files and preferences through an iOS app-group container. This is between app components on the same device, not by itself sharing with other users or a developer server.

5. Analytics, advertising and system diagnostics

Voce does not integrate advertising, advertising-identifier tracking, or a third-party analytics/crash-reporting SDK that automatically sends your usage behavior to the developer. We do not sell your app data or share it for advertising targeting.

Apple may process crash reports, test feedback or store statistics under its own systems and your diagnostics/TestFlight choices. Apple’s policies and settings govern those activities; they are not a cloud processing service for your audio or notes.

6. Subscriptions and the App Store

Purchases, renewals and restoration are handled by Apple’s App Store and StoreKit. The app processes Apple-verified product and transaction information, subscription periods and revocation/refund status to determine Pro access. Voce does not receive your payment-card details.

Purchases and some entitlement checks may require internet access. Voce does not attach your audio or note text to subscription transactions. Apple’s payment and account processing is governed by its own policies: Apple Privacy Policy.

7. Backups, export and third parties

Depending on your iCloud or computer backup settings, app data may be included in device backups. Your device and backup settings control those copies. The absence of automatic uploads to a Voce content server does not mean that no device backups exist.

Exported files are prepared on your device. Choosing to save or share through Files, email, messaging or another app transfers content to that destination. That service’s privacy, retention and internet-use practices apply. Deleting content inside Voce does not delete copies you previously exported or sent to other people.

8. Retention, deletion and security

Notes, source files and recovery data remain on your device as needed for app functions. Notes moved to Trash can be restored until permanently deleted. Deleting only audio keeps the transcript. Permanent deletion removes the relevant note and associated app files; external copies and earlier device backups must be managed separately.

Temporary export copies used for sharing/preview have a one-hour age threshold. Cleanup runs when the app starts, and iOS can also remove temporary files. This is not a promise of immediate or exact one-hour deletion. Permanent deletion is not a guarantee of forensic erasure.

Local files use iOS file protection. Protecting your device and backups remains important; no storage method guarantees absolute security. Removing the app does not automatically remove existing device backups or copies you shared.

9. When you contact support

The email address, message and any screenshots/files you choose to send may be processed to respond to your request and investigate the issue. Share only necessary information; sending an audio recording is not required for ordinary use. Support correspondence is retained only as needed to resolve the request and meet applicable obligations. Your email provider’s terms also apply.

10. Your controls and requests

You can view, correct, export and delete notes in the app, and revoke microphone permission in system settings. Because we cannot remotely access content held only on your device, we cannot delete those files for you from a server.

For access, correction or deletion requests under applicable data protection rules, or questions about support correspondence, contact the address above. Requests are assessed against the data actually under our control and the applicable rules.

The app is a general-purpose note tool and is not designed specifically for children. You are responsible for obtaining appropriate permissions when recording or sharing other people’s voices.

11. This web page

When this policy is hosted on GitHub Pages, GitHub stores visitors’ IP addresses in server logs for security purposes. This page has no developer analytics, advertising, external fonts or tracking scripts. Opening it does not upload your notes to GitHub. Details: GitHub Privacy Statement.

12. Changes

If the app’s data handling changes, this policy will be updated and its last-updated date revised. Notice will be provided in the app or by another appropriate method when needed. This text covers the current practices described here; it is not permission for undisclosed data collection by future features.
