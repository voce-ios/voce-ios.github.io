Restore a purchase

Open Voce, go to Settings → Plan & subscription, then tap Restore purchases. Use the same Apple Account you bought Voce Pro with.

Cancel or change a subscription

On the plan screen tap Manage subscription, or open Settings → your name → Subscriptions on your device. Cancel at least 24 hours before the end of the current period to stop the next renewal.

Free plan

Recording has no time limit and the full audio is always kept. The free plan transcribes the first 1 minute 30 seconds of each recording. Voce Pro transcribes the whole recording and adds export, sharing and AI drafts.

AI summaries are not available

Summaries use Apple Intelligence on your device. They need a supported device, iOS version, language and region, with Apple Intelligence turned on and ready in Settings. Recording, transcription and editing work without it.

Apple Watch

Open Voce on your Apple Watch and tap Record. The audio is sent only to your paired iPhone and becomes a note there. If the iPhone is out of reach, the watch keeps the recording and sends it later.

Your data

Recordings and notes stay on your device; Voce has no account and no server. Privacy policy: https://voce-ios.github.io/privacy/ · Terms of use: https://voce-ios.github.io/terms/
